Welcome to Lesotho, a country with many names, The Mountain Kingdom, also The Kingdom in the sky.It is called the kingdom in the sky becuse it's land area lies above 1000 meters(3 281 feet) in elevation, with it's highest low point sitting at approximately 1400 meters(4593 feet) avbove sea level.
Lesotho is completely landlocked in Southern Africa, with a 2026 population of about 2.15-2.3 million. Lesotho is a country is a country with mixed cultures, for example, Basotho,Xhosas,Zulus and many other which goes to show why Lesotho is considered the land of peace.
